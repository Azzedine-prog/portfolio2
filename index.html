About Me

I am Azzedine Lakhdar, an embedded software engineer at Lear Corporation specializing in automotive electronics and ECU development.

I design, integrate, and validate AUTOSAR-compliant software stacks, blending rigorous functional safety practices with pragmatic delivery. From CAPL scripting to Azure DevOps pipelines, I enjoy building reliable embedded solutions and mentoring teams on best practices.

Outside work I keep experimenting with robotics, Linux-powered systems, and side projects that bridge hardware with connected services.
